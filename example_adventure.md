Closer inspection reveals that the object upon which the man is fiercely focused is a chess board. As you draw nearer to the man in his seat, he looks up from the board and waves you over.

"Hello there, Bimmy McJimson. I don't believe we've met."

Slightly alarmed, but also intrigued, you take a step forward. "You know my name?" you ask.
